Steel Front

4 v 4 tank battle. Germans against Americans and Soviets, with AI crews filling the other seats. Pick your tank.

W/S drive, A/D steer hull, mouse aims the gun, click fires, hold right mouse for the gunsight. Marks on the sight show shell drop at 400 m steps. Hit sides and rears; frontal armor on heavies is very hard to defeat.
